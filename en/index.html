About

Professional summary

Product Manager with 10+ years building digital products across retail, consumer apps, B2B SaaS and logistics.

Most recently at Grupo Boticário, parent company of O Boticário, the world's largest beauty retailer by number of stores (Euromonitor), with nearly 4,000 stores across 16 countries. I led a team of 5 PMs on the company's proprietary logistics platform, which surpassed R$50M in annual recurring revenue and grew processed GMV by 258% year over year. Before that, I took its last-mile delivery app from zero to 1 million orders per month in under a year.

At Magazine Luiza, one of Brazil's largest retailers with 1,200+ stores and a leading e-commerce marketplace, I led a product and engineering squad at Jovem Nerd, the group's pop-culture media brand, relaunching a website that reached 4.5M monthly users.

I do my best work in ambiguous, multi-stakeholder environments, where decisions need to be grounded in data and tied to business impact.

I use AI-native tools daily to prototype, validate and ship faster. They shorten the path from idea to evidence and let me bring working prototypes, not just documents, into conversations with engineering.
